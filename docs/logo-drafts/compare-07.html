draft4, at every size

draft4 against the shipping mark (01) and last round's corrected letterform (06), rendered at true pixel sizes rather than downscaled from 512. Regenerate with node build-07.js.

Short version. draft4 is the best small-size performer here — its counters are 55.5% / 63.1% wide against 01's 46.0% / 57.9%, and counters are what decides 16px. It is also the only mark in the exploration whose waist goes the wrong way (84.8%, against the 64–67% that 06 and Bricolage hold), which is defensible only because draft4 is a different concept — a page whose right edge is two bowls, rather than a letterform with a corner cut. It cannot ship as exported: hardcoded fill, off-grid geometry. See section 6.

2. the top-right corner

draft4, top-right corner, measured

red: the ink left between the counter's tip and the 45° cut. green: the top bar, every other bar in the mark.

The upper counter is not a closed bowl. Its right boundary runs out to a curved point at (341.5, 152.8) and back to (257, 68), so the ink between that tip and the 45° cut narrows to about 48 units where every other bar in the mark is 68 and the stem is 96.

The obvious accusation — that the pinch turns to grey mush at small sizes — does not hold up when measured. Counting pixels that land neither as ink nor as background in the mark's top-right quadrant: at 16/20/24/32/48px draft4 runs 21/18/23/13/9% against 01's 17/20/21/14/11%. All three marks carry a 45° diagonal through that corner, so they all antialias about the same, and draft4 is if anything the cleanest by 48px. The pinch is thin, not blurry.

The real objection is shape. Because the counter's boundary is convex into the corner, the leftover wedge reads as a crescent — a pennant, a flag — rather than as a folded triangle. A dog-ear flap has straight edges; this one bulges, and it comes to a point at the top instead of meeting the cut squarely. Whether that is a bug depends on whether the corner is still meant to say “folded page”. It reads as deliberate ornament at 48px and up, and it is simply gone below 24px, which is the same place 08 landed: the fold is a 512px feature either way.

Same measurement method for every row: rasterise to 1024 tall, take the outer width of each scanline, and read the minimum between the two counters.

draft4 has the shallowest waist in the entire exploration — 84.8%. 01 was already flagged at 74.5% for welding the two bowls into one mass down the right side, and 06 was cut specifically to bring that to 64%. draft4 moves it 10 points the other way.

Whether that is a defect depends on which mark draft4 is. As a letterform it is a regression on the one number last round settled. As a page whose right edge happens to be two bowls — which is what the straight left edge, the two 46.5-unit corner radii and the straight bottom actually draw — a shallow waist is the point, because a deep notch would stop the silhouette reading as a card. draft4 is a different concept, not a worse execution of the old one, and it should be judged as one.

What is not in tension: draft4's counters are much larger (55.5% / 63.1% wide against 01's 46.0% / 57.9%), and that is what keeps it open at 16px. Counters are the thing that decides the small end — the same finding the 04* studies landed on.

4. app icon masks

All three marks at full bleed and at 82%, under the masks the platforms actually apply. The dashed circle is the Android maskable safe zone (80% diameter); anything crossing it can be cropped.

draft4 does not move the maskable number. Measuring the circumradius about the tile centre, all three marks fit the safe circle at a mark height of 64.2% of the tile — identical, because they share the 0.863 aspect and proportionally identical corner radii (46.5/512 against 4/44). The 82% row above is over that budget for every one of them, which is the finding compare.html already carried; draft4 neither helps nor hurts it.

6. what has to change before this can ship

  • The fill is hardcoded #101828. The shipping mark is one path in currentColor so it composes on any surface and needs no theme token — see the comment at the top of bracemark-icon.tsx. draft4 as exported stays near-black on a near-black background. This is the same failure 04-draft2.svg had.
  • The geometry is a tool export, not a construction. 46.5098, 0.000270318, 465.454. The 442×512 box is 11.6364× the 38×44 grid, so nothing lands on a round number. Whatever wins gets re-cut on one grid before it goes into bracemark-icon.tsx — bracemark-expo's port copies the numbers by hand.
  • The middle bar is heavier than the horizontals — 80 units between the counters against 68 for the top and bottom bars. Conventionally a B's middle bar is equal or slightly lighter.
  • The upper counter's pointed lobe — section 2.
  • The ink bbox is exactly the viewBox, with the lower bowl touching the right edge at x=442. Correct for a source file; it just means padding is entirely the caller's job, and the caller currently is nine PNG sizes plus three iOS variants.
