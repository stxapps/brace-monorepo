waist reach — how far right the silhouette still reaches where the two bowls meet, as a share of full width. Lower is a deeper, more pinched waist. stem — width of the left stem as a share of glyph width, measured at 22% height. ink — share of the bounding box that is filled.

01-baseline sits inside Bricolage's range on w/h, stem and waist height, and outside it on waist reach by 11 points. 04b matches the bowls but its stem is thinner than Bricolage's lightest weight while its bowls are heavier than its heaviest.

4 · the lockup that actually ships

apps/bracemark-site/src/components/site-header.tsx:28 puts the mark at 28px and the word Bracemark in font-display — Bricolage, font-semibold (600), tracking-tight — 10px to its right. So a drawn B and a typeset Bricolage B sit side by side in the header of the apex domain. That is why the mark has to agree with the face.

The same two B's at matched cap height — the mark's B beside the wordmark's B, which is the comparison the header is making 10px apart.
